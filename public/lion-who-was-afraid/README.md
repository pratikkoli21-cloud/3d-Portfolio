# The Lion Who Was Afraid

A wholesome 15-second vertical YouTube Shorts **video** for young children.

- Video: [`the-lion-who-was-afraid.mp4`](./the-lion-who-was-afraid.mp4) — 1080×1920, 9:16, 15 seconds, with music, meows, and dialogue

## Story

| Time | Moment |
|------|--------|
| 0–3s | Confident lion walks the savanna, hears a tiny “MEOW!”, and freezes |
| 3–8s | He hides his big body behind a small tree while the camera zooms in |
| 8–12s | A tiny kitten steps out of the grass and meows; the lion realizes |
| 12–15s | Embarrassed lion: “Oh… THAT was the scary sound?” Kitten meows, lion jumps, freeze-frame |

Same lion and kitten throughout. Family-friendly comedy — no violence or scary imagery.
